Privacy Policy

Effective the day the Founding Traveler List opened

1. What we actually do with your data

Tree & Ember collects Founding Traveler List signups solely to send launch updates and new-entry notices. We do not sell, rent, or trade anyone's information, and we don't run it through advertising networks.

2. What we collect

When you join the Founding Traveler List, we collect your name, email address, and the topic you told us draws you in (cartography, bestiary, oral history, or trade & factions). That's the whole list.

3. How long we keep it

Your entry stays on the list until launch notices stop going out or you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first.

4. Contact & inquiries

For questions about this policy or to be taken off the list, reach out through our official contact channels once they're published alongside launch.
